Why Nookab

Most vocabulary apps hand you someone else's word list. Nookab starts with yours — the words from your books, your shows, your conversations. Every word you practise is one you chose to learn.

How it works

From “what was that word?” to mastered.

01

Collect the words you meet

Type the word and its translation, pick a word type, done. Add plural, example sentences, synonyms and notes whenever you want more. Type “der Hund” and Nookab files the article for you.

02

Let AI do the busywork Optional

Turn on AI Auto-Fill with your own free Gemini key and one tap suggests translations, grammar and examples for you to review. It's off by default, and your word goes straight from your phone to Google — never to us.

03

Practise in short bursts

Flashcards, a four-option quiz or spelling from memory. Sessions run from 5 to 100 questions and can be narrowed to any category — Travel, Food, Work, whatever you made.

04

Watch them become yours

Every answer moves a word's mastery score. Get it right across modes and days, and it earns its trophy. Get it wrong, and Nookab brings it back sooner.

05

See how far you've come

Your statistics show 14 days of activity, accuracy by word type and category, your longest streak, and the words that still give you trouble — so you know exactly what to practise next.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something missing? Email us — a real person reads every message.

What is Nookab?

An offline-first vocabulary notebook for people who want to practise the words they actually meet. Instead of a pre-built course, you add words from your own reading, watching and conversations — then practise them with flashcards, quizzes and spelling.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every practice mode, chart and setting is available from day one. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no locked features.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Adding words, every practice mode, statistics, streaks, reminders and Excel import/export all work in airplane mode. The only feature that needs internet is the optional AI Auto-Fill.

Which languages can I learn?

German or English — you choose one when you start. English learners get past tense and past participle for verbs and comparative and superlative for adjectives; German learners get color-coded articles, plurals, genitive and full conjugations. German learners can also switch the app interface to German. AI Auto-Fill can translate into English, German, Bangla, Spanish, Portuguese or French.

How does Nookab decide what I practise?

Each session mixes weak, learning, practicing and mastered words (roughly 40/30/20/10), with the words you're most likely to forget first. Unlike strict spaced-repetition apps, it never blocks you from practising any word.

What is AI Auto-Fill?

An optional helper, off by default. With your own free Google Gemini API key, one tap suggests a word's translation, grammar, examples and more. Only the word you typed is sent — directly from your phone to Google, never to us. Always give the suggestions a quick check.

How do I move to a new phone?

Go to Settings → Import & Export and export your vocabulary as an Excel file. Import it on the new phone and your words, mastery and practice history come with it. Since everything lives on your device, this file is also your backup.

Who made Nookab?

An SQA engineer learning German, who kept losing new words to notebooks and scattered apps. Every feature in Nookab solves a problem from that journey. It's built and published by Hanan Atelier.
