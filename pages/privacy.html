Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Nookab ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our vocabulary learning application.

Nookab is designed to be offline-first and privacy-preserving by default. The Developer operates no server, no account system, and no analytics back-end, and does not collect, receive, or store your personal information. All user data accessed by the App is processed strictly locally on your device. Information leaves your device only in two cases, both initiated by you: (a) when you manually export your vocabulary to an XLSX file, and (b) when you choose to use the optional AI Auto-Fill feature, which sends the single word you typed directly from your device to Google's Gemini API using your own API key. The Developer receives none of that information. AI Auto-Fill is off by default and is entirely optional.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Privacy Policy have the meanings assigned to them in the Terms and Conditions.

2. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

Except for the optional AI Auto-Fill described in §5.3, the App uses purely on-device access and processing, and the data listed below remains within your exclusive physical possession and control.

2.1 Data You Input and Manage

The App stores the following data locally within its private sandbox directory:

  • Vocabulary Data: The words and phrases you manually add, including their translations, definitions, example sentences, parts of speech, and grammatical attributes
  • Practice History: Records of your practice sessions, including correct/incorrect answers, response times, and mastery levels per word
  • Profile Information: The display name, greeting title (if any), and profile photo you choose to set (profile photos are accessed via the secure native system picker and copied directly to the App's local sandboxed directory)
  • App Preferences: Your language settings, notification preferences, theme selection, and other configuration choices
  • AI Auto-Fill Settings: Whether AI Auto-Fill is enabled, your fill mode, your preferred target translation language, and the number of translation variants you selected. Your optional Gemini API key is stored separately in encrypted operating-system secure storage (see §4.1) and is never placed in the vocabulary database.

All of the above data is created and controlled entirely by you. No data is stored or processed without your direct, manual action.

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

The Developer deliberately does not collect, view, receive, or store any off-device information. The only information that ever leaves your device does so at your direction and goes to the recipient you choose — Google's Gemini API for the optional AI Auto-Fill (§5.3), or the destination of an XLSX export (§5.2) — never to the Developer. The information we do not collect includes:

  • Personal Identifiers: No email addresses, phone numbers, real names (unless voluntarily typed into your local profile), or government-issued IDs
  • Account Credentials: No usernames, passwords, or Nookab authentication tokens — the App features no cloud account or registration system. Your optional Gemini API key is not a Nookab account credential; it is stored only on your device (see §4.1)
  • Location Data: No GPS or geolocation information, and the Developer performs no IP-based tracking. Note: when you use AI Auto-Fill, your device's IP address is necessarily visible to Google as the recipient of your request; the Developer never sees it (see §5.3)
  • Device Identifiers: No device IDs, advertising IDs (GAID), IMEI numbers, serial numbers, or unique hardware identifiers
  • Usage Analytics: No remote telemetry or background tracking of how you use the App
  • Automated Crash Logs: No automatic collection or transmission of crash reports, diagnostic data, or performance metrics
  • Browsing History: The App itself does not contain a general-purpose web browser, nor does it deploy cookies, web beacons, or tracking pixels
  • System Permissions: No access to your contact lists, camera, microphone, or social media graphs
  • Financial or Biometric Information: No payment card data, bank details, or biometric logs (Face ID/Fingerprint information remains managed strictly by your mobile operating system)

2.3 Automatic Data and Ephemeral Network Requests

The App does not automatically collect any data. No telemetry, analytics SDKs, usage reporting components, background diagnostic scrapers, or advertising components are built into the application code, and the App performs no background network activity.

The App makes outbound network requests only when you explicitly trigger them:

  • AI Auto-Fill: when you tap "Auto-Fill" in the word form, and when you tap "Validate & Save" while adding your own Gemini API key. These requests go directly from your device to Google's Gemini API, never to the Developer. See §5.3.
  • Opening a web page: the App may open your device's web browser to a Google AI Studio page so that you can create or manage your own API key. That page is loaded by your browser, not by the App.

When you manually submit a support request or bug description via email, we will receive only the technical details and information you explicitly choose to include in that email communication. We strongly advise against sending sensitive personal information in support emails.

3. How Your Data is Used

Except for the optional AI Auto-Fill described in §5.3, all processing is confined to your local hardware, and we do not process, view, analyze, or exploit your data in any way. Data remains on your device solely to facilitate the App's core features:

  • Vocabulary data powers your personal dictionary, search engine, and review cycles
  • Practice history enables spaced-repetition logic and progress calculation
  • Profile details personalize the localized interface layout
  • Preferences establish user-configured interface behaviors
  • AI Auto-Fill settings determine whether, and how, the optional AI Auto-Fill feature behaves

No Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning Training by the Developer: We do not transmit, sell, or use any of your locally stored data—including vocabulary entries, custom definitions, or practice history—for artificial intelligence training, LLM fine-tuning, machine learning model development, automated profiling, or algorithmic analysis. This prohibition applies to the Developer. The optional AI Auto-Fill sends only the single word you typed to Google's Gemini API to generate suggestions for that word; the Developer neither receives that request nor uses it to train any model. Google's handling of that request is governed by Google's terms (see §5.3).

4. Data Storage, Security, and Destruction

4.1 Sandboxed Local Storage

All data is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database (nookab.db) within the App's isolated, secure application sandbox on your device storage. Profile photos are stored as isolated files inside the App's protected local document directory. The API key you optionally provide for AI Auto-Fill is not stored in this database; it is stored in the operating system's secure credential storage via expo-secure-store (backed by Android Keystore and iOS Keychain) in encrypted form accessible only to the App. The Developer never receives your key. Under standard mobile operating system security protocols, this sandbox isolates the App's data so that other applications installed on your device cannot access it.

4.2 Data Retention & Destruction

Your data remains stored on your device as long as the application remains installed. Data persists safely across normal app updates. You maintain full execution control over data destruction via the following methods:

  • Granular Removal: Deleting specific vocabulary definitions or practice parameters directly within the interface
  • Bulk Clear: Executing "Reset Practice Data" or "Delete All Words" options inside the Settings menu
  • AI Auto-Fill Key and Settings: Deleting your stored Gemini API key, or disabling AI Auto-Fill, from Settings → Preferences → AI Auto-Fill at any time
  • Full Data Obliteration: Uninstalling the application entirely from your device. Uninstallation triggers an immediate and permanent deletion of the application's entire private sandbox directory by the operating system, including your stored API key. Because this data is never synchronized with an external server operated by the Developer, the Developer cannot recover your data once the app is uninstalled.

Deleting your API key stops future AI Auto-Fill requests originating from your device. It does not delete any content that Google may have retained from earlier requests, which is governed by Google's own retention practices and terms (see §5.3).

4.3 Device-Level Security Measures

Because data storage is entirely local, the security of your information relies on your physical device security infrastructure. We strongly recommend securing your smartphone using a strong device passcode, PIN, or biometric lock, keeping your mobile operating system updated, and activating full-disk device encryption.

4.4 Software Vulnerability Disclosures

Because the Developer does not operate any remote database and does not receive the contents of AI Auto-Fill requests, a network-side data breach affecting the Developer is not possible. The security of requests handled by Google's Gemini API is governed by Google's security practices and terms (see §5.3). However, if a software vulnerability is identified within the App code that could expose the local database sandbox or your stored API key to other malicious software on a device, we will disclose this transparently via in-app update banners and through the Google Play Store release notes, alongside immediate instructions on installing the corresponding security patch.

5. Third-Party Libraries and Data Sharing

5.1 Third-Party SDK Disclosures

The Developer does not sell, rent, lease, trade, or share your data, and receives none of your locally stored data. The App contains no commercial third-party advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, programmatic ad servers, or telemetry aggregators.

5.2 User-Initiated Data Sharing (XLSX Export)

The App provides a built-in XLSX export tool that compiles your local vocabulary entries into a standard spreadsheet file. When you trigger an export, you initiate an on-device transfer to a destination of your choice (such as local file directories, device memory, or an external communication app). Once exported, this file leaves the security boundaries of the App's sandbox. You are solely responsible for securing, sharing, or encrypting your exported XLSX files.

5.3 Optional AI Auto-Fill via Google Gemini API

AI Auto-Fill is an optional, off-by-default feature. It is not required to use the App, and every core feature — manually adding words, the practice modes, statistics, and XLSX import/export — works fully offline without it. To use it you must take two affirmative steps: enable AI Auto-Fill in Settings and supply your own Google Gemini API key.

What is transmitted. When you tap "Auto-Fill" in the word form, the App sends a request directly from your device to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com). That request contains:

  • the single word you typed in the form (with internal whitespace normalized);
  • your learning language;
  • the number of translation variants you selected;
  • your selected target translation language for the translated fields; and
  • your API key, used to authenticate the request.

The request does not include your name, profile photo, other vocabulary entries, practice history, statistics, device identifiers, or any other locally stored data. The App does not transmit your database, or any portion of it, to Google or to the Developer. Separately, when you tap "Validate & Save", the App sends a lightweight request to Google's models endpoint to confirm that your key is accepted; that request contains your API key but no vocabulary content.

Who receives it. The request is sent to Google, not to the Developer. The Developer operates no server, proxy, or relay for this feature and receives no copy of the request, the response, or your API key. Your device's IP address is necessarily visible to Google as the recipient of the request.

Google's role and terms. Google acts as an independent third party (and, where applicable, an independent data controller) for the processing of AI Auto-Fill requests. Google's handling of that information — including retention, security, international transfers, and any use of API content to improve Google's products and services (which may apply depending on the tier or account associated with your API key) — is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the applicable Google API and Gemini API terms, not by this Privacy Policy. The Developer does not control and is not responsible for Google's processing. You should review Google's terms before enabling the feature.

Accuracy. AI-generated results may be incomplete, inaccurate, or inappropriate. They are suggestions only. You are responsible for reviewing and correcting anything the feature fills in before you save it.

Your choice. You can disable AI Auto-Fill or delete your stored key at any time (see §4.2). Doing so stops all future AI Auto-Fill requests from your device. You may also obtain, restrict, or revoke your API key through your Google account.

5.4 Open-Source Software Dependencies

The App integrates open-source framework libraries solely to facilitate local system functionality. These packages operate strictly within your device environment and do not themselves transmit data or generate outbound network pings, except for the network request you explicitly initiate under §5.3 (which uses the standard React Native networking layer):

  • Expo Core SDK (including expo-sqlite, expo-secure-store, expo-sharing, expo-document-picker, expo-file-system)
  • React Native Framework (and community layout utilities including react-native-svg, react-native-reanimated, react-native-safe-area-context, react-native-screens, expo-router)

6. Your Rights and Global Compliance

Because Nookab processes all data on-device, you enjoy direct, unfettered access to your information without needing to file a data disclosure request. For the optional AI Auto-Fill, the Developer receives no data to disclose; those requests are made by your device directly to Google.

6.1 GDPR (EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland Compliance)

  • Data Controller: The Developer acts as the data controller for the purpose of engineering the software. However, because data processing occurs entirely within your device hardware under your active execution, you retain operational control over your data. For the optional AI Auto-Fill, the Developer is neither a controller nor a processor of the request — your device sends it directly to Google, which acts as an independent controller for its own processing. The Developer does not receive it.
  • Legal Basis: Local processing is based on your consent provided during onboarding and is strictly necessary to perform the service requested (Articles 6(1)(a) and 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation). For AI Auto-Fill, the lawful basis is your consent, given by enabling the feature and supplying your own key; you may withdraw that consent at any time by disabling the feature. Google's processing is governed by Google's own legal terms.
  • Your Rights: You can fully exercise your rights to Access (Art. 15), Rectification (Art. 16), Erasure (Art. 17), Restriction of Processing (Art. 18), and Data Portability (Art. 20) instantly by interacting with the app's local settings, updating entries, or deleting the application. Because the Developer holds no copy of AI Auto-Fill requests, any request relating to content processed by Google should be directed to Google under Google's privacy terms.
  • Complaints: You maintain the right to file a formal complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (Art. 77 GDPR), though we invite you to email us directly first to address any operational bugs.

6.2 CCPA/CPRA (California, USA Compliance)

  • Right to Know & Delete: You hold complete visibility into your data directly through the interactive user interface. You may delete all locally held attributes at any time using the in-app wipe features or by performing a full uninstallation. Because the Developer receives no AI Auto-Fill data, there is nothing for the Developer to disclose or delete; you can stop future requests by disabling the feature or deleting your key.
  • No Sale or Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share consumer personal information with third parties. No "Do Not Sell My Info" mechanisms are required because no such tracking or monetizing activities take place. The optional AI Auto-Fill is a request you direct to Google, not a sale or sharing of personal information by the Developer; the Developer receives no data and no consideration for it.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not targeted at or designed to attract children under 13 years of age, and the Developer does not knowingly collect, harvest, or request personal data from any user, including minors. The App works offline, and the optional AI Auto-Fill requires the user to obtain and enter their own Google API key — a step that, under Google's terms, requires an eligible Google account and is not intended for children under 13. The Developer therefore does not execute behaviors restricted under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. If a minor uses AI Auto-Fill, that request is governed by Google's terms.

If you are a parent or guardian and discover that a minor has entered personal information into the App, you can completely purge that local data immediately by deleting the App from that child's device.

8. International Jurisdictions & Cross-Border Transfers

Because the App functions primarily offline and the Developer never transfers data off your hardware, no international data transmissions or cross-border data routing take place with respect to the Developer. The one exception is the optional AI Auto-Fill: when you use it, your request is transmitted directly from your device to Google's Gemini API, which may process it in data centers located outside your country, including in the United States, under Google's terms and safeguards (see §5.3). XLSX exports go only to the destination you choose.
